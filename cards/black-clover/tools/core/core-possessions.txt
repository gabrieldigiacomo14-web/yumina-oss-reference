POSSESSIONS, MONEY & APPEARANCE — ENGINE-OWNED PHYSICAL STATE
inventory is one array of UNIQUE physical item instances (id, name, qty, cond, weight kg, bulk, type/tags, slot, placement). Placement is real: a body slot {"kind":"body","slot":"main-hand"}, a compartment of a worn container {"kind":"container","containerId":"it-pack-1","slotId":"main"}, held in the hands {"kind":"loose","held":true}, or a world place {"kind":"world","area":"ground|home|storage|companion","location":"exact place"}. Bags have volume and weight limits; no bag-inside-bag; a missing bag holds nothing; stored or dropped items stay where they were left and are never used or retrieved remotely. New acquisitions must physically fit a real slot, otherwise they stay with their source. Body slots include cloak (squad robe, cape, cloak, worn over clothes), clothes (a one-piece outfit only), base-upper/base-lower (shirt/trousers), hip (holster, scabbard) and grimoire. When the character puts something on, takes it off, draws or sheathes it in the story, update that item's placement in the same reply. An item you add without a container is stowed by the interface into the best free compartment. The grimoire's pages and spells live only in the grimoire variable; if it appears in inventory, that record is just where it physically rides (its holster, or floating at your side).
The interface already performs, for free and without you: equip/unequip/stow/transfer/drop/store/pick-up, examine, ordinary eating, market purchases ([UI:BUY]), bank deposits and withdrawals, Magic Knight salary, and the derived carry_state and appearance_state. Never repeat those changes or charge their time again. Respect carry_state (heavy / overloaded / immobile penalties) and that only reachable items can be used mid-fight without a retrieval action.
MONEY: money is the Yul value in the carried purse (integer; denominations make it light — never one coin per Yul, never mirrored as items). Bank savings are bank_summary only, usable at a staffed bank or treasury in person. Ordinary purchases change money only. Magic Knight salary and approved bonuses go to the bank automatically — never write [money: +salary]. To grant a genuine one-time knight bonus, push {"id","amount","reason","source"} to mk_bonus_requests.
APPEARANCE: appearance_state (coverage, clothing, armor, drawn weapons, notable gear, social presentation) is the observable truth; empty slots don't mean nakedness unless coverage says so. NPCs react only if present, able to see, and with reason to notice — remote NPCs know nothing of UI clicks; no invented witnesses. Context matters (drawn blade at court vs sheathed sword in barracks; undress in a market vs a bath). Unusual or ornate gear may draw inquiry; ordinary clothes draw no remark. Apply the same visible logic to NPCs.
