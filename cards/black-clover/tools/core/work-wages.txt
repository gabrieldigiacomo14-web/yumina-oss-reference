WORK & EMPLOYMENT — YUL ECONOMY (deterministic payroll/treasury modules are authoritative)
Jobs are persistent positions: employer, occupation, location, agreed wage, schedule, colleagues, duties, advancement. Hiring depends on skill, magic, availability, class prejudice, demonstrated reliability and real openings. No guaranteed work; wages are paid once, only for a genuine contract or completed work, from the employer's funds. Monthly Magic Knight pay is credited by the engine, not by you.
CANON [C]: Yul is Clover currency; a new knight like Asta receives about 200,000 Yul on his first payday (Ch.10 / Ep.11). Rank and merit affect pay and privileges. Per the Vol. 6 Q&A, even the lowest Magic Knight earns more than an ordinary citizen's average ANNUAL income — a strong inequality constraint, not a published civilian wage table. Ranks: Junior, Intermediate and Senior tiers, each Fifth → First Class, then Grand Magic Knight and Wizard King.
SIMULATION PAY SCALE (monthly base, Yul): Junior 5th 200,000 · 4th 215,000 · 3rd 230,000 · 2nd 250,000 · 1st 275,000. Intermediate 5th 310,000 · 4th 350,000 · 3rd 395,000 · 2nd 445,000 · 1st 500,000. Senior 5th 575,000 · 4th 660,000 · 3rd 760,000 · 2nd 875,000 · 1st 1,000,000. Grand Magic Knight 1,200,000. Equal base across squads is a simulation policy. The Wizard King's resources are state appropriations, not a rank salary. Captain and vice-captain are appointments (possible duty allowances), not classes.
MERIT: each confirmed PERSONAL merit star adds 1% of rank base (cap +15%) — a simulation formula. Squad gold/black star totals change team standing and budgets, never personal pay; black stars can bring investigations and discipline, never arbitrary pay deductions. A captain may authorize a genuine, funded ONE-TIME bonus: push {"id","amount","reason","source"} to mk_bonus_requests — never also credit money. Star awards themselves are not cash.
PAYROLL: service starts when mk_status.squad and mk_status.rank are set; the engine anchors pay to that date (no retroactive windfall), credits the bank at each monthly anniversary, skips duplicates, survives squad transfers, and pauses on leaving. You may narrate a captain handing over a payslip, but never write [money: +salary].
CIVILIAN INCOMES (gameplay reconstruction, not canon): peasant, day-labor and low-wage town households ≈3,000–10,000 Yul/month in cash; craftspeople and steady urban workers ≈8,000–16,000; skilled mages, merchants and nobles far more. Subsistence farming, barter, room-and-board, family wages, patronage, bad seasons and regional prices all matter. A junior knight's monthly pay dwarfs many workers' yearly cash and often supports a whole family; noble knights may have family wealth but get no higher base for bloodline.
PURCHASING POWER: 1 Yul ≈ ¥1 of nominal price intuition only (not an exchange rate or a modern economy). Supply, monopolies, war, magic inputs, scarcity and barter often break parity (see Economy, Prices & Trade). Show economics through family expectations, requests for support, taxes, dependence on powerful houses and career choices; nominal yen-like prices don't make peasants comfortable.
