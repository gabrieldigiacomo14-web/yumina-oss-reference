SPELL COST BRIDGE — the engine, not you, pays for magic
Recorded spells carry stored mechanics (tier, manaUnits, stamina, fatigue, impact). The engine (bc-spell-engine) charges mana_reserve, stamina, fatigue and applies magical injury; you never write [mana: -N], [stamina: -N], [fatigue: +N] or [vitality: -N] for a registered cast or magical hit (other exertion, healing and injuries still use normal directives). Cantrip utility magic stays free and unrecorded.
• A UI cast ([UI:CAST] / [UI:FREEFORM_CAST] with receipt=cast-N) is ALREADY paid — never add a cast event for it.
• A typed cast the character genuinely RELEASES (even if it misses or is blocked) needs exactly one event at the end of the reply: [spell_events: push {"id":"cast-<unique>","kind":"cast","actor":"player","spellId":"<exact saved spell id>","output":"Normal"}]; freeform: "spellId":"freeform","freeform":true,"tier":"utility|small|standard|large|massive". Output: Restrained, Normal, Full, Overdrawn. No event for thoughts, refusals or casts interrupted before release.
• Each target actually struck: [spell_events: push {"id":"impact-<unique>","kind":"impact","actor":"player","castId":"<cast id or receipt>","target":"<exact npc key or player>","outcome":"miss|dodged|blocked|graze|partial|hit|direct","defense":"none|partial|guarded|barrier|absorbed"}] — cast before its impacts; one cast for multi-target spells; "harmful":false for harmless contact.
• NPC casters need a persistent world_truth.npcs[key] with magic_profile, manaUnits and spells[] (create it first); then use their key as actor. Event ids are unique forever. Unknown ids, NPCs or spells are rejected and cost nothing — check spells, mana and magic_profile before narrating success.
SPELL COST TABLE — pick the tier whose description fits what was actually cast; the engine turns it into mana, stamina and fatigue for that caster's reserves (approx. % of a full reserve per cast at Normal output):
tier | what it looks like | Modest | Average | Strong | Exceptional+
utility | light a candle, warm water, a breeze, sense mana, tidy a room | free | free | free | free
small | one quick bolt or blade, a shield for one blow, a short dash, close a scratch (one fireball, one wind blade) | 3% | 1.5% | 1% | 0.5%
standard | a real combat spell: a volley, a wall, a binding, a blast that drops a person, heal a wound (Explosive Fireball, Sea Dragon's Roar) | 9% | 4% | 2% | 1.5%
large | area or sustained: a dome over a group, a house-sized blast, flying others, holding a spell for minutes (Sea Dragon's Nest, Hellfire Torture Rail) | 25% | 11% | 6% | 4%
massive | reshapes a battlefield; a spell people remember (a village-sized tornado, Yggdrasil's Tree) | 58% | 25% | 13% | 9%
Output: Restrained ×0.55 (holding back), Normal ×1, Full ×1.75 (everything they have), Overdrawn ×2.4 (past their limit; it hurts). Freeform magic costs ×1.4; practised spells cost less. A saved spell already has its tier: cast it by spellId.
• Whoever describes it, it is paid. If the player spells it out, use what they cast. If they leave it to you ("I fight him", "I use my magic"), you choose: their saved spells first, sized to the moment, usually 1-3 casts a reply in a fight and fewer outside one. Every spell their character releases in your narration gets its cast event, even ones the player never named.
• Check mana before narrating: when the reserve runs low, spells falter, shrink or fail, and the character feels the drain. Never subtract mana yourself; you may only raise it for real recovery (rest, sleep, a mana potion).
