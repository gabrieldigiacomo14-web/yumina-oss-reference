MAGIC CORE LAW (details load when relevant)
Mana lives in beings and in nature. A mage's power is multi-axis: potential, capacity, current mana, output, control, recovery, sensing, spell control, casting speed, concentration. Big reserves ≠ strong attacks; control can beat raw power. Attribute (what mana IS: Fire, Wind, Steel…) ≠ Form (what magic DOES: creation, reinforcement, healing, restraint, trap, curse, sealing…) ≠ Spell (a grimoire-recorded expression). One attribute per person unless a real cause exists (hybrid ancestry, devil host, soul phenomena, artificial modification, explicit sandbox).
ONE SYSTEM: spells, forms, magic tools, chores, strikes and exam demonstrations all draw the same mana through the same personal stats. Freeform evaluation against the character's own profile is the DEFAULT; a recorded spell is a freeform expression practiced until it repeats cheaply and fast. Powering a broom or tool is magic directed into an object (weak output = a low, wobbling broom).
A mage's attribute is usable directly at any time, grimoire or not; talent and training set how far it goes (Yuno uses wind without his book; an untrained person's raw magic is small and rough). The grimoire amplifies, stabilizes and formalizes — it is the source of neither mana nor skill.
Grimoires are soul-bound: they choose their owner (usually at fifteen at the March ceremony in a grimoire tower; latecomers can visit a tower later), float and open to the right page, stop responding when too far from their owner, vanish at the owner's death, and can't be sold or used by others. Clover's are three-leaf; four-leaf is extraordinarily rare; five-leaf is a devil-linked world event, never a ceremony roll. Spells are never bought, levelled or granted for time served: they emerge through intentional shaping, organic development and breakthrough, and the page appears the moment it happens (write it to spells that turn). Canon spells belong to their canon owners.
Mana is spent for real, scaled by output, size, range, duration, complexity and instability, reduced by efficiency and proficiency; low mana degrades control and output; at zero there is no meaningful casting and collapse is possible. Cantrip-tier utility (sweeping with wind, drawing water, lighting a lamp) is UNTRACKED — no cost, no directive, and narrated as effortless as breathing. Combat-tier and larger spells are the real economy: breathlessness, sweat and fatigue scale with what is actually spent.
Special powers keep their own rules: Anti-Magic is never an attribute or a pick; Mana Skin, Mana Zone, Mana Method, Ki, True Magic, Ultimate Magic, spirit and devil power must be learned or arise from real circumstances. Magic Stages are a Heart Kingdom assessment, not a universal level system.
NPC theory is never universal law: a trainer's metaphor or drill is characterization, but ordinary casting runs on the mage's OWN mana alone — no touching the ground, circuits or drawing on nature's mana. If an explanation of basic magic starts to sound like Mana Zone or Mana Method, that IS those gated techniques; never teach their mechanics to an ungated character under another name. Terrain matters tactically, never as a casting requirement.
Attribute interactions are physical tendencies (water douses fire, lightning conducts through water, wind feeds flame), never type-chart guarantees: output, density, construction, control and environment decide every collision.
